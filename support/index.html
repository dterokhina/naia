Your account and data

Do I need an account?

Nope. There’s no sign-up, email or password. Open the app, add the date your last period started and you’re done.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, inside the app. We don’t have a copy and we can’t see it. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I move to a new phone?
  1. On your old phone, go to Settings → Copy all my data.
  2. Paste it somewhere you can get to from the new phone, like a note or an email to yourself.
  3. On the new phone, go to Settings → Restore and paste it in.
  4. If your subscription doesn’t show up, tap Restore purchases.

Because we don’t keep a copy, we can’t get anything back from a lost phone. It’s worth making a backup every now and then.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete all my data, or just delete the app. Either way it’s gone for good, and we can’t undo it.

Paying

How much is it?

The first 7 days are free. After that you pick monthly or yearly. You’ll see the price in the app before the trial starts, and you won’t be charged during it.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then Naia.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

On Android

  1. Open Google Play and tap your profile picture.
  2. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then Naia.
  3. Tap Cancel.

If you don’t want to pay, do this at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle refunds, so we can’t do it from our side, sorry.

  • iPhone: go to reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: find the order in Google Play and tap “Request a refund”

Using Naia

How accurate is it?

It’s a best guess based on what you log, and it gets better after a few cycles. If your cycle is irregular it’ll be less sure of itself.

Is it medical advice or contraception?

No to both. Naia can help you spot patterns, but it’s not a medical device and you shouldn’t use it to avoid pregnancy. For anything health-related, talk to your GP.

My reminders aren’t showing up
  1. Check notifications are switched on for Naia in your phone’s settings.
  2. Make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb isn’t hiding them.
  3. In Naia’s Settings, turn the reminder off and back on.
