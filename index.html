Questions

How much is it?

The first 7 days are free. Then monthly or yearly. You’ll see the price before you start, and you won’t be charged during the trial.

Do I need an account?

Nope. No sign-up, no email, no password.

How accurate is it?

A best guess from what you log. It gets better after a few cycles. Please don’t use it as contraception.

How do I cancel?

In your phone’s subscription settings. Do it at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you don’t want to pay.
