Sound familiar?

  • One week I’m on fire. The next I can’t answer a single email.

  • I plan a huge week, then crash by Wednesday and feel like a failure.

  • My meds just seem to stop working the week before my period.

For a lot of women with ADHD, that up-and-down follows the menstrual cycle. Naia helps you see the hard days coming and plan around them, so they stop catching you off guard.

How it works

01

Tell it when your last period started

That’s the whole setup. No forms, no account. Naia works out where you are in your cycle and when your next period’s due.

02

Check in when you remember

Tap a bubble for focus, mood and energy. Small means low, big means high. It takes about 20 seconds, and missing a day is totally fine.

03

Plan around your energy

Got a tax return or a call you’ve been dreading? Put it on a strong day. Hard days get rest and one small thing. If today’s not happening, tap “Not today”.

Every month, you go on a dive

Estrogen helps your brain use dopamine, which ADHD brains are already low on. When it drops before your period, focus and patience often drop with it. Drag across the days to see.

What do you want help with?

It’s the first thing Naia asks you. Tap one to see what it does.

Naia marks your Good days, so the big stuff lands when you’ve got the most in the tank.

Questions

How much is it?

The first 7 days are free. Then monthly or yearly. You’ll see the price before you start, and you won’t be charged during the trial.

Do I need an account?

Nope. No sign-up, no email, no password.

How accurate is it?

A best guess from what you log. It gets better after a few cycles. Please don’t use it as contraception.

How do I cancel?

In your phone’s subscription settings. Do it at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you don’t want to pay.
